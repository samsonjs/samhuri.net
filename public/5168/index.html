A 1947 rancher tucked into the trees on an acre and a quarter in Cordova Bay, a few minutes from Elk Lake.

The house is bright and open, with a wood-burning fireplace in the living room and an updated kitchen that opens onto a big back deck. Outside there are mature gardens, fruit trees, a fire pit, lawns and a meadow, plus a detached one-bedroom cottage and a separate art studio.
