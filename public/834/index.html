A bright northwest corner suite on the top floor of The 834, with floor-to-ceiling windows on two sides and a balcony that wraps around both of them. You get sunrises and sunsets most of the year, and views over the city to the harbour, the Sooke Hills and Mount Baker.

Ceilings are over nine feet, the floors are laminate, and the living and dining space is one open room facing the view. The kitchen has quartz counters, plenty of cabinets and stainless steel appliances, including a two-year-old dishwasher. There's in-suite laundry, the bedroom has a wall unit and a walk-through closet to the bathroom, and the bathroom has heated floors. Hot water is included, and the rent comes with a parking stall with its own bike rack, and a storage locker.
